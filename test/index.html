    Click the board for fullscreen. Left and right arrow keys change pages. L opens the logbook, R the radar, D the connection log. Status shows Overhead (directly above, within the overhead distance), Inbound (on course to pass over within three minutes), Flying by (getting closer but won't pass over), Outbound (moving away) or Passed. Every plane that comes within the spotting radius is added to Spotted today; the list marks the ones that flew directly overhead, and otherwise shows Arriving or Departing for your home airport. Browsers only allow sound after a first click or key press on the page. Live data: adsb.lol and airplanes.live. Routes: adsbdb. Logos: the free airline logo service from Aviasales; when one is missing the airline code is shown instead.
